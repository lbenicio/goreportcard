About

Go Report Card was initially developed during Gopher Gala 2015. It is free and open source. If you feel like there is something that could be improved, open an issue or contribute on GitHub.

Credits

Current maintainer: lbenicio.

The Go gopher on the home page was designed by Renée French.

Go Report Card uses the Go toolchain and open-source analyzers including gocyclo, ineffassign, and misspell.
